About Tongmeng

Our Goal

We are a registered non-profit organization located in BC, Canada that is on a mission to educate students interested in STEAM fields by providing a rich selection of free STEAM courses run by our dedicated instructors. We seek to bring free, valuable STEAM education to everyone and bring together people who share the same passions in STEAM fields.

Our Story

Tongmeng Learning Society is a non-profit organization located in BC, Canada that is dedicated to providing children with a wider range of learning opportunities through cooperative learning. We have assembled a team of middle/high school students who will be offering free STEAM classes designed to stimulate elementary school students' interest in science, technology, engineering, art, and math while providing them with inspiring learning experiences at the same time. With our team at Tongmeng Learning Society, we hope to create a vibrant, supportive, and friendly learning community.
