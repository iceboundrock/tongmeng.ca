Beginner C++ Course

Instructor: Peiling Li
Dates: Dec. 27 - Feb. 7
Time Frame: 5-6 PM PST.
Recommended Grade(s): 6+

C++ is an object oriented programming language that is widely used for developing software applications, including games and browsers. It can be used in a variety of platforms, such as Windows, Linux, etc. This course serves to introduce students to the basics of C++, and at the end of the course, they will be able to code in basic C++.

Science

Dates:
Time Frame: PST.
Recommended Grade(s): 7 and below

A science course designed for students in grades 7 and below offers an engaging exploration of fundamental scientific concepts. The curriculum covers topics such as biology, physics, and earth sciences. Students are encouraged to ask questions, make observations, and draw conclusions, fostering curiosity and a foundational understanding of the scientific method.
