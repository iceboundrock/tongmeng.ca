Free Courses in STEAM!

Start Learning STEAM Today!

Tongmeng Learning Society is a non-profit organization located in BC, Canada that is dedicated to providing children with a wider range of learning opportunities through cooperative learning. We have assembled a team of middle/high school students who will be offering free STEAM classes designed to stimulate elementary school students' interest in science, technology, engineering, art, and math while providing them with inspiring learning experiences at the same time. With our team at Tongmeng Learning Society, we hope to create a vibrant, supportive, and friendly learning community. Start learning today!

Sign-Up for our courses!

We offer a variety of courses in STEAM like coding, math, and more! All these courses are run by our experienced volunteer instructors and hope to educate interested students in STEAM from beginner to advanced levels.
